Fulfillment Economics Protocol

FEP is Luzione and Bravi's matched checkout layer. A customer may add a small contribution to an order, choose a Bravi Rewards route, and receive an update after eligible value is allocated.

This is not only charity

The goal is capacity return. Bravi Rewards helps initiate useful projects that can serve small businesses, communities, and restoration needs. Bravi may help pay for part of a project, coordinate the service work, supply labor or operators, and receive service revenue when the project is completed. That revenue can then be distributed through the Bravi Rewards system and used to support future capacity.

What customers choose

  1. Small business capacity: helps fund useful work for smaller operators and project partners.
  2. Community projects: helps initiate service projects requested by real communities or partners.
  3. Environment restoration: helps initiate cleanup, repair, or restoration work where feasible.

How checkout matching works

  1. The customer chooses a 2.5% or 5% contribution before checkout.
  2. Luzione matches the customer contribution.
  3. The order records the selected Bravi Rewards route and return preference.
  4. After the order is confirmed, eligible value is queued for allocation, service planning, or project support.
  5. If the customer requests an update, Bravi/Luzione can send the fulfillment or allocation status when available.

Returns and unused value

If an order is returned before the contribution has been allocated or work has begun, unused eligible value may be returned, kept in Bravi Rewards, or held until the customer confirms reallocation, depending on the preference selected at checkout and the applicable return policy. Work already completed is not clawed back from people, operators, or project partners.

Why this matters

Traditional giving often ends as a disconnected donation. FEP is designed as an auditable commerce layer: customer match, Luzione match, Bravi Rewards route, allocation record, service outcome, and return logic. The long-term objective is to make commerce fund useful work while preserving transparency, accountability, and economic return.
