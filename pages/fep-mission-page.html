Choose your visibility.

  • PrivateOnly authorized operational participants can see the activity.
  • Giver + recipientThe people involved can see it; the public cannot.
  • Public aggregateOnly gift count, value, and reconciled status are included.
  • Public attributedAn opted-in alias may appear. Need evidence, contact details, provider references, and redemption links never do.

How the backend is divided.

  • Bravi RewardsPoints, wallets, reservations, gift-card orders, and delivery instructions.
  • FEP PlatformPrograms, funding rules, eligibility, allocations, human decisions, audit, and public impact.
  • Sultan-FEPBounded recommendations only—no approval, spending, final recipient selection, contact, or publication.
  • TremendousDelivery provider for approved gift-card orders and lifecycle status.

Essentials and work items.

Verified catalog items may use Amazon Associates links. Any qualifying affiliate earnings go to an aggregate FEP funding pool. Using a link is optional and does not earn personal points, create donation credit, change eligibility, or improve priority.

Bravi Rewards points are not cash and gift-card availability may vary. Gifts to individuals are generally not tax-deductible. Recommendation is not approval; approval is not provider submission; provider submission is not delivery or reconciliation.
